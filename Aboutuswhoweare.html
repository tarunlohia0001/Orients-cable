About Us

Since 2002, Orient Cables has been a top manufacturer and exporter of an extensive range of cables, including Optical Fiber Cables, Networking Cables, CCTV Cables, Coaxial Cables, Control & Instrumentation Cables, Power Cables, Hybrid, Special Purpose Cables tailored for specific applications, and Moulded Assemblies. With a team of dedicated professionals and experts in R&D, Product Development, QC, and Manufacturing, Orient Cables has built an unparalleled reputation and acceptance in Indian and Global markets. Our cable manufacturing unit boasts of the following:

  • Over 50, mostly imported, extrusion lines working on PVC, LSZH, PE, TPE, TPU and many automated, precision machines for various operations working tirelessly 24×7.
  • Independent fully equipped quality testing labs regularly testing raw materials, in process and finished products to meet the most updated industry domestic and international standards, such as BIS, UL, ETL, VDE, MIL, BS, CE, and DIN.
  • Over 32,000 Sq. Mtrs of Factory with close to 300,000 Sq. Ft of covered production, stores and office spaces.
  • Annual CAGR from the last 20 years exceeding 35%.

As a leading cable manufacturer in India, we also export our products to USA, UAE, Israel, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, Nepal, Bhutan, Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and many other countries and have a local presence in Dubai.

Logistics

Orient is committed to providing efficient logistics solutions to its customers. Our dedicated and experienced support team coordinates all aspects of order processing, manufacturing, material handling, and shipping to ensure a quick turnaround time, regardless of whether the product is custom-made or a standard item. We value every customer and recognize the importance of customized packaging requirements. Our team takes great care to ensure that products are packed and shipped according to national and international standards, guaranteeing damage-free delivery to the customer’s warehouse. Additionally, we offer real-time shipment tracking, allowing customers to monitor their shipment’s progress every step of the way.

Manufacturing capability

Our sprawling 8-acre facility embodies the concept of lean manufacturing, ensuring speedy product delivery. With strict quality control processes, we guarantee reliability, performance, and affordability, providing repeated opportunities for customer satisfaction.

Innovation, R&D , QA

Orient has emerged as a leader in innovation, design, and development, fueled by the drive to replace costly imported cables with our own indigenous solutions. Our dedicated team of skilled professionals is deeply involved in every phase of product development, ranging from design and testing to the creation of cutting-edge manufacturing processes. Through the utilization of the latest tools and methodologies, we ensure that our product prototypes align with customer expectations in terms of quality, safety, functionality, and budget. Moreover, we adhere to stringent parameter tolerances in accordance with both national and international standards.
