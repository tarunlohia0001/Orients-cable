On our path to reducing the carbon footprint, we are committed to continuously reducing the carbon footprint in our operations. To reduce the carbon footprint, we have been continuously working since 2020 by using solar panels and uses of battery-operated material handling equipment.

By using the solar panel, we have saved 505 MT of CO2 till May 2023 in the last 3 years.

In continuation of this journey, In 2023, Orient Cables has initiated the complete evaluation of the CO2 footprint at the business level and will continue the reduction of CO2 by setting time bounded goal and targets.
